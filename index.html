Mga estimate

Water System Calculators

Tiyaking tama ang units. Estimate lamang ang resulta, hindi engineering design.

1. Flow volume

Formula: L/min × minutes.

2. PSI ↔ bar

1 bar ≈ 14.5038 PSI.

3. Static water head

1 m water head ≈ 0.0981 bar. Hindi kasama ang friction loss.

4. Pipe internal volume

Gamitin ang actual internal diameter, hindi nominal size.

5. Rectangular tank volume

Formula: haba × lapad × taas ÷ 1,000 = liters.

Hindi pump-sizing calculatorKailangan sa pump selection ang required flow, total dynamic head, source level, friction losses, outlet pressure at manufacturer pump curve.
